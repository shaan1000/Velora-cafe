Our story

Started with one grinder and a quiet corner.

Slow is a choice.

Velora began in 2019 when two friends turned a neglected garden-side shopfront into the café they wished existed: honest coffee, real baking, and no rush.

Today we work with small estates across Karnataka and Kerala, bake every morning, and still hand-pour every filter.

Source

Direct trade with 6 estates, paid above market.

Craft

Every cup is dialled in daily by our baristas.

Community

Open mics, book swaps and a table for anyone.
